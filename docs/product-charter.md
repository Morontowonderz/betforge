# BETFORGE product charter — BF-002
Status: design proposal for local research, not commercial clearance.

## Purpose and users
Build explainable football intelligence for analysts and research-oriented football followers. User segments and willingness to pay are hypotheses, not validated customer demand. The product must expose data quality and uncertainty, including no-signal outcomes.

## Initial scope
Local research and paper trading only. Evaluate full-time Over 1.5 Goals and full-time BTTS as distinct primary markets. First-half counterparts require separate market identifiers, rules, training/evaluation and settlement. Seventh-minute evaluation is a hypothesis, not a proven opportunity. Minute 5, 10 and 15 comparisons require a preregistered multiple-testing procedure.

## Non-goals and prohibitions
No real wagers, fund transfers, bookmaker-account management, private-interface scraping, guaranteed returns, public tips, billing, customer messaging, paid infrastructure or production deployment. No copying unrelated project code. Each regulated or externally consequential operation needs specific human approval after impact/cost review.

## Product evidence
Before implementation claims: executable tests and independent review. Before statistical claims: timestamp-safe chronological holdouts, baseline comparisons, calibration, uncertainty and complete accepted/rejected/missing decision records. Before commercialization: verified data rights, jurisdictional legal review, responsible-use controls and evidence of useful customer outcomes. Prediction failure may justify a descriptive analytics product, never unsupported wagering claims.

## Success and stop conditions
Foundation success: reviewed contracts, provider-rights evidence or explicit blockers, preregistered research and reproducible verification plan. Customer discovery targets: 15 interviews, five qualified prospects, three serious design partners; two paid pilots only after legal clearance. These are targets, not achieved results.
Stop acquisition if permitted use is unresolved; stop signals if freshness, fixture identity, market mapping or model quality is invalid. Stop a subsystem after two failures on one root cause. Do not cross failed phase gates.

## Open owner/legal decisions
Target customer jurisdictions; age restrictions; commercial data distribution model; acceptable operating budget; meaning and intended use of prediction delivery. No assumptions here constitute legal advice or approval.

## Acceptance checklist
Research-only mode, primary markets, user hypotheses, prohibited operations, evidence requirements, stop conditions, commercial targets and unresolved decisions are explicit. Document completeness is not customer or legal validation.
